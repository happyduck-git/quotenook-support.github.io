Questions

Where is my writing stored?
Only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
Can I see it on another device?
Not yet. Syncing between devices is being worked on.
I cannot find a book.
Try the author or the ISBN instead of the title. If it still does not show up, add it with "Enter manually".
What is Premium?
A one-time purchase that unlocks widget styling and export. On a new device, "Restore Purchase" brings it back.
